## 1. Project Overview

**Vatan.uz** is an interactive educational platform designed for students in grades 9–11. It provides a structured and engaging learning experience focused on Uzbekistan, its history, national identity, social development, and digital responsibility.

The platform combines educational content, interactive activities, reflection exercises, and presentation-oriented features in one cohesive experience.

Its central theme is:

> Turli millat va elatlar — yagona Vatan, umumiy kelajak sari.

The project aims to encourage students to understand their country's past, evaluate its present, and think critically about its future.

## 2. Key Features

### 📚 Structured Educational Content

Five dedicated learning sections:

- **01 — Yagona Vatan:** National unity, cultural diversity, shared values, and social harmony.
- **02 — Mustaqillik:** Uzbekistan's independence, historical milestones, national institutions, and sovereignty.
- **03 — Islohotlar:** Social and economic reforms, education, healthcare, digitalisation, and entrepreneurship.
- **04 — Kiberxavfsizlik:** Digital literacy, password security, phishing awareness, and responsible internet usage.
- **05 — Xulosa:** Research activities, survey results, personal reflections, and key takeaways.

### 🌐 Multilingual Interface

The platform provides three language options:

- Uzbek
- English
- Russian

### 🎯 Interactive Learning

- Scenario-based cybersecurity exercises.
- Knowledge-oriented activities.
- Personal reflection fields.
- Interactive educational elements.
- Section-based learning progression.

### 📊 Research & Survey Activities

- Demonstration survey data.
- Real-data entry mode.
- Visual presentation of survey results.
- Opportunities for classroom research and discussion.

### 🎤 Presentation Mode

A presentation-oriented experience designed to support classroom demonstrations and educational discussions.

### 🔎 Additional Learning Resources

- Search functionality.
- Frequently Asked Questions.
- Reference library.
- Explanatory glossary.
- Dedicated project information section.

### 💾 Browser-Based Persistence

The platform does not require an account. User settings and certain activities are stored locally in the browser.

## 9. Known Limitations

- No account-based synchronisation.
- Browser-local persistence does not automatically transfer between devices.
- Demonstration survey results are illustrative and must not be presented as genuine research findings.
- Some educational statistics require verification against current official sources.
- Local installation requirements remain to be documented.
